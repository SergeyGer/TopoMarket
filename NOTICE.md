# Data notices and attribution

TopoMarket is research software. The repository contains **no private market data**: the demo
profile generates synthetic fixtures locally, and the private seed archive is excluded by
`.gitignore` and never committed.

When using the private profile, the owner supplies the third-party data. The following sources are
the documented providers for the research dataset; their terms apply to any use, storage or
publication of genuine data, charts, screenshots or derived tables:

| Source | Provider | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily S&P 500 history | OpenIntro / Yahoo Finance | Historical daily index observations |
| S&P 500 price index | S&P Dow Jones Indices via FRED | Reproduction/distribution may require prior permission |
| Spot VIX history | Cboe Global Markets | Official daily spot-index archive |
| 12 US industry portfolios | Kenneth R. French Data Library | Daily value- and equal-weighted returns; retrospective releases |
| Financial Stress Index | Office of Financial Research (OFR) | Signed stress index with a reporting lag and revisions |

Synthetic demo data is generated by the project itself and is marked as synthetic in filenames,
manifests, UI labels and validation tables. Do not present synthetic data as market history.

For the full import contracts, units, join rules and provenance requirements see the TopoMarket
technical specification maintained with the private research inputs (not part of this repository).
