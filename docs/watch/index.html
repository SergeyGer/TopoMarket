What you are watching

  • Structure. Twelve industry portfolios are nodes; an edge means their trailing 60-session returns correlate at ρ ≥ 0.80. The film contrasts dated synthetic windows, then opens the Mapper view of shared portfolios.
  • Warning. A separate score embeds 60 daily S&P 500 log returns in three delay coordinates (56 points), measures the longest persistent H₁ loop, and ranks its deviation from trailing history as a percentile. The threshold is frozen before evaluation.
  • Replay and evidence. Blind Replay hides the outcome until you reveal the next 20 sessions. The public holdout is synthetic, so the honest result is weak: TDA warned before 0 of 3 episodes, VIX before 1 of 3. See the evaluation rules and the validation evidence.
